# Part 3 — Narrative Prompt Pack

## Trigger

Generate a concise business narrative when a category shows a meaningful month-over-month revenue change.

## Input List

The narrative uses:

- Category name
- Previous month
- Current month
- Previous revenue
- Current revenue
- MoM percentage
- Status
- Relevant business context
- Audience

## Prompt

Write a concise business narrative explaining the detected revenue movement.

The narrative must contain:

1. Context — identify the category, months, and revenue movement.
2. Insight — state the observed numerical change as a fact.
3. Implication — explain what the change may mean for business attention.
4. Action — suggest an appropriate next step without inventing unsupported causes.

Do not invent reasons for a revenue change. Clearly distinguish observed facts from hypotheses.

## Checklist

Before accepting the narrative, check:

- [ ] The category is clearly identified.
- [ ] The relevant months are clearly identified.
- [ ] The MoM percentage is numerically correct.
- [ ] The observed revenue movement is stated as a fact.
- [ ] Any possible explanation is clearly presented as a hypothesis.
- [ ] The narrative is specific to the intended audience.
- [ ] A practical next step is included.
- [ ] No unsupported facts or causes are invented.

## Quality Criteria

The narrative should be evaluated for:

- Specificity
- Audience fit
- Completeness
- Actionability
