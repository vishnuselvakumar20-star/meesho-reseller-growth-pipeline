# Part 3 — Chart Decisions

## 1. Highest Monthly Revenue

### Recommended Chart
Bar chart

### Justification
A bar chart is appropriate for comparing revenue across categories or months because the values are discrete and the main purpose is to compare their magnitudes.

---

## 2. Ethnic Wear Revenue Share

### Recommended Chart
Pie chart

### Justification
A pie chart is appropriate when showing the share of Ethnic Wear revenue within the total revenue for a single month. Each slice represents a category's contribution to the whole.

---

## 3. Region Comparison

### Recommended Chart
Bar chart

### Justification
A bar chart is appropriate for comparing revenue across regions because each region is a separate category and the relative revenue values can be compared directly.

---

## Chart Selection Principle

The chart type is selected according to the structure of the data and the intended analytical question rather than using the same chart type for every dataset.
